About Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB)

Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) is Malaysia's leading integrated electricity utility company, responsible for the generation, transmission, and distribution of electricity to millions of consumers across the country. As a major player in the energy and utilities sector, TNB is committed to modernizing its operations through advanced technology solutions and digital transformation initiatives.

Engagement Context

TNB recently upgraded to Pega Platform version 25.x and is actively exploring advanced features including Infinity Studio and AI Assistant capabilities in version 26. This session was part of their digital transformation roadmap, enabling their technical and business teams to understand how AI-powered development tools can accelerate their application development and implementation capabilities.

What Went Well

Key Highlights

  • Prep Call: Prep call with account team was handy to understand audience profile and streamline presentation.
  • Audience Engagement: Audience was very engaging and received Infinity Studio very well.
  • Feature Reception: CLI was the feature that got applauded most.
  • Team Support: Presence of John and support by other Pega team members in the room really helped navigate through audience questions.

Delivery Observations

Session Format & Structure

  • Effective Format: Format of the session with setting context through couple of slides followed by hands-on demo really works well. Slides help understand the depth of knowledge audience possesses.
  • Remote Facilitation Challenge: It's hard to contribute and control a bigger group remotely as Emcee.
  • Agenda Planning: Having a full agenda upfront, especially if this session is part of a full-day engagement, would help organize the session better.

Areas for Improvement

Recommendations for Future Sessions

  • Moderator Coverage: For larger groups (>5 participants), have more than one moderator in the room to make it easier to attend to different questions simultaneously.
  • Time Management: Time checks from remote location can get tricky when important tangent conversations are ongoing in the room. Establish clear signaling mechanism with in-room team.
  • Agenda Coordination: Agenda should be shared with Demo X organization in advance if this session is part of a full-day plan with client.

Audience Feedback

Client Interest & Next Steps

During the Q&A and closing discussions, Tenaga Nasional expressed strong interest in understanding the upgrade timeline to Infinity Studio version 26 and how they can leverage these new AI-powered functionalities for their development teams. There was active discussion around implementation roadmap and resource planning for the upgrade.

Status: Detailed feedback from Tenaga Nasional participants is pending collection. A QR code to register feedback was shared with all participants at the end of the session and will be collected and analyzed. This section will be updated once feedback is received and compiled.

Action Items & Next Steps

1. Debrief the Team: Team debrief happening as part of current meeting (Demo X organization internal session)
2. Upskill Team Members: Upskill more team members to take up Emcee and Logistics roles for future client sessions
3. Share Feedback Analysis: Share feedback with wider team regarding effectiveness of in-person vs. remote roles for this type of session format

Lessons Learned

Key Insights

  • Slides Set Context Effectively: Current slides really help in setting the context to the audience, making technical concepts more accessible and digestible.
  • Moderator Capacity Limits: Having 1 moderator for more than a group of 4 wouldn't really work. Multiple moderators needed for larger audiences to manage engagement and Q&A effectively.
  • Time Distribution Needs Review: Due to audience questions and high interest around Implementation tasks and AI Assistant, we may not get enough time to showcase UI Designer and CLI features. Time allocation should be re-visited for future sessions based on audience priorities.
  • Diverse Role Composition Works Well: Having a group within different roles (technical developers, business stakeholders, SC, CSM) for the session works effectively and provides varied perspectives during discussions.
  • Extra Environments Critical: Having extra environments can come in really handy if participant numbers exceed what was anticipated. Ensures smooth hands-on experience for all attendees.

Recommendations for Future Sessions

  • Clear Agenda: Have a prep call with the account team if this session is part of a full-day program. This ensures alignment on objectives, timing, and handoffs between sessions.
  • Moderators in the Room: Always have moderators in the room to make the session more interactive. If needed, have more than one moderator to manage larger groups and ensure better audience engagement.
