Client Information

Organization: MUFG

Industry: Insurance

Business Context: At MUFG, secondary loan sales and syndication distribution requests are currently processed through labor-intensive, manual email inbox monitoring. This decentralized, high-touch process results in extended processing turnaround times, increases operational risk, and constrains our ability to scale with market demand. Transitioning toward automated intake and workflow routing will accelerate execution speed, eliminate operational bottlenecks, and elevate client service levels. Pega Agentic Email autonomous ingestion directly converts unstructured email trades into structured, governed enterprise cases with Straight-Through Processing. Through our DemoX collaborative build, MUFG can experience a live 26.1 Agentic Email environment packaged into an interactive leave-behind web asset—validating the immediate business impact and operational time-to-value.
