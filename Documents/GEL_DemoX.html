Recommendations for Future Sessions

  • Plan longer time for Blueprint Hackathon — half-day format is too short for meaningful deliverables
  • Use terminology closer to participants' working knowledge (e.g., SOP instead of casetype/workflow) to improve accessibility
  • When time is limited, focus more on workflow lifecycle to maximize business value delivery
  • Highlight personas in the workflows, so users can see which persona is involved in each part
  • Assign table champion to facilitate. Showcase blueprint in one-go instead of breaking it into parts.
