MUFG Agentic Share Sale Experience

How DemoX converted a constrained request into a client-ready Infinity 26.1 experience

📋 OVERVIEW

Business Problem

MUFG secondary loan sales and syndication distribution requests were processed through labour-intensive, manual email inbox monitoring. This decentralised, high-touch process extended turnaround times, increased operational risk and constrained the ability to scale with market demand.

Project Scope

Showcase intelligent email intake, document extraction, share sale journey via agentic email and chat agent, missing-information handling and controlled compliance review.

Pega Solution

Pega Agentic Email autonomous ingestion converts unstructured email trades into structured, governed enterprise cases with Straight-Through Processing.

📊 Experiential Proof

Through the DemoX collaborative build, MUFG could experience a live Infinity 26.1 Agentic Email environment packaged into an interactive leave-behind web asset, validating immediate business impact and operational time-to-value.
